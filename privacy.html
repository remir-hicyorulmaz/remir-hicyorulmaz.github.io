Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Kanal Planlayici is a private desktop application used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

Data the application accesses

  • Account information for the owner's connected accounts: account ID, username and display name.
  • Content information for videos published on those accounts: video IDs, links, captions, titles, descriptions, publish status and time.
  • Performance numbers for those videos: views, reach, likes, comments, shares and saves.
  • Access tokens issued by Google, Meta and TikTok after the owner authorizes the application.

The application does not access data of any other users, followers or commenters, and does not read private messages.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner a content calendar and performance numbers, and to publish videos and edit titles or captions on the owner's accounts when the owner approves it.

Storage and sharing

  • All data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner's user account.
  • No data is sent to any server other than the official APIs of Google (YouTube), Meta (Instagram) and TikTok.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as it is needed for the purposes above. The owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the application's local data folder. Access can be revoked at any time from the account settings of Google, Instagram or TikTok, after which the application can no longer access the account.
