Kanal Planlayici

Kanal Planlayici ("channel planner") is a private desktop application that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used to plan, approve and publish short science and history videos on the owner's own channels:

  • Why On Earth Science — YouTube, Instagram (@whyonearthscience) and TikTok (@whyonearthscience)
  • Blueprints of Madness — YouTube, Instagram (@blueprintsofmadness) and TikTok (@blueprintsofmadness)

What it does

  • Shows a weekly content calendar for both channels across YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
  • Lets the owner review, edit and approve each post (caption, publish time) before anything is published.
  • Publishes approved videos to the owner's accounts through the official YouTube, Instagram and TikTok APIs.
  • Reads basic performance numbers (views, likes, comments, shares) for the owner's own videos.

Who can use it

Kanal Planlayici is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, and it connects only to accounts the owner controls. There is no sign-up, no other users and no advertising.
